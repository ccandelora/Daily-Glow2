# Daily Glow - App Store Assets

## App Description
Daily Glow is your personal companion for mindfulness and emotional wellness. Track your mental well-being through interactive daily check-ins and guided reflections designed to help you build healthy habits and strengthen your emotional resilience.

With Daily Glow, you can:
• Set personalized wellness goals like reducing stress, improving sleep, and increasing mindfulness
• Track your daily mood patterns and emotional health with an intuitive interface
• Build and maintain healthy streaks to encourage consistency
• Earn achievement points that celebrate your wellness journey
• Receive customizable notifications for daily check-ins and wellness tips
• Monitor your progress with visual insights that help you understand your emotional patterns

Daily Glow's calming interface and science-backed approach to mental wellness help you develop greater self-awareness and emotional intelligence. Make mindfulness a daily habit and watch your emotional well-being transform over time.

Your mental health journey is personal—all your data stays private and secure. Start your path to emotional wellness today with Daily Glow.
