Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

To reset your password, go to the login screen and tap "Forgot Password." Enter your email address, and we'll send you instructions to create a new password.

Why did I lose my streak?

Streaks are maintained by completing a daily check-in each day. If you miss a day, your streak will reset. However, we provide a 24-hour grace period, so you have until the end of the next day to complete your check-in before losing your streak.

How do notifications work?

You can customize your notification preferences in Settings. We offer daily check-in reminders, achievement alerts, and wellness tips. You can enable or disable each type and set preferred times for reminders.

Can I change my wellness goals?

Yes! You can update your wellness goals at any time in Settings. Tap on "Your Goals" to select from options like reducing stress, improving sleep, building healthy habits, increasing mindfulness, boosting mood, and tracking mental health.

How is my data protected?

Your privacy is important to us. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your personal information. Your check-in data is stored securely and is never shared with third parties. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account, go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Please note that this action is permanent and will remove all your data from our systems.

App Troubleshooting

General Troubleshooting Steps

  1. Ensure your app is updated to the latest version
  2. Close and restart the app
  3. Check your internet connection
  4. Restart your device
  5. If problems persist, try uninstalling and reinstalling the app

Known Issues

  • Notification delays - Some devices may experience delayed notifications. This is often resolved by ensuring your device's battery optimization settings are not restricting the app.
  • Profile picture upload failures - If you're having trouble uploading a profile picture, try using a smaller image or checking your internet connection.
